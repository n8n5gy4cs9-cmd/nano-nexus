# Mixing basics

## Compression

A compressor reduces the difference between loud and quiet parts.

### Threshold and ratio
The threshold is the level where compression starts. The ratio says how strongly signals above it are reduced: 4:1 turns 4 dB over the threshold into 1 dB.

### Attack and release
A slow attack lets the start of each note through (punchy). A fast release makes the level recover quickly after the signal drops, which can sound pumpy on full mixes.
