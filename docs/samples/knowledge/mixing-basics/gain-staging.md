# Mixing basics

## Gain staging

Gain staging means setting the level at every step of the signal chain so nothing clips and nothing is buried in noise.

### Input level
Record with peaks around -12 dBFS. A hotter signal does not sound better in a 24-bit recording, and it leaves less headroom for plugins.

### Master level
Keep the master meter peaking below -3 dBFS while mixing. Leave loudness to the mastering step.
